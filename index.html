The Story Behind Dramatherapy Bay

A bay to pause. A space to explore. A way forward.

A bay is a sheltered place where boats can find a moment of calm, take refuge from the open water and prepare for their next journey.

This is the idea behind Dramatherapy Bay.

Dramatherapy Bay takes inspiration from this idea: a welcoming space to step back, reflect, explore what matters and consider where you might wish to go next.

Our Services

Different ways to explore, express and grow.

We offer a range of therapeutic and creative services to support individuals, groups and organisations. Each approach is shaped around the needs, experiences and goals of those we work with.

Individual & Group Dramatherapy

A supportive space to explore emotions, relationships and life experiences through conversation, creativity and expression.

Creative Wellbeing Workshops & Training

Tailored, experiential workshops for universities, schools and organisations, supporting emotional wellbeing, self-expression, connection and personal development.

Specialized Treatment

Expert care for eating disorders, trauma, and complex mental health needs.

Professional Development & Creative Coaching

Reflective and creative approaches to personal and professional development, navigating transitions and exploring new possibilities.

Bespoke Projects & Partnerships

Collaborative creative wellbeing projects developed with educational institutions, charities and community organisations.
