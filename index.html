Dramatherapy Bay

A space to pause, explore and find your way forward.

Dramatherapy and creative psychological support in Cambridge and online. At Dramatherapy Bay, we believe that not everything can be expressed through words alone. Through conversation, creativity and embodied exploration, we offer a thoughtful space to make sense of experiences, explore emotions and discover new perspectives. We work with individuals, groups and organisations, offering tailored therapeutic support, creative wellbeing workshops and collaborative projects.

The Story Behind Dramatherapy Bay

A bay to pause. A space to explore. A way forward.

A bay is a sheltered place where boats can find a moment of calm, take refuge from the open water and prepare for their next journey.

This is the inspiration behind Dramatherapy Bay.

We see therapy as a space where you can step back from the demands of everyday life, reflect on what matters and explore different ways of understanding your experiences.

Our Services

Different ways to explore, express and grow.

We offer a range of therapeutic and creative services to support individuals, groups and organisations. Each approach is shaped around the needs, experiences and goals of those we work with.

Individual & Group Dramatherapy

A creative and relational approach to exploring emotions, relationships and life experiences. Through conversation, stories, imagery, role-play and other creative processes, dramatherapy offers different ways to express, reflect and make meaning.

Learn More →

Creative Wellbeing Workshops & Training

Tailored, experiential workshops for universities, schools and organisations, supporting emotional wellbeing, self-expression, connection and personal development.

Learn More →

Specialist Eating Disorder Support

Psychological support for people experiencing eating difficulties and eating disorders. Depending on individual needs, support may include dramatherapy, Cognitive Behavioural Therapy for Eating Disorders (CBT-T) and Guided Self-Help (GSH).

Learn More →

Professional Development & Creative Coaching

For individuals navigating professional challenges, transitions and personal development. Explore perspectives, recognise existing strengths and consider new possibilities.

Bespoke Projects & Partnerships

Collaborative creative wellbeing projects developed with educational institutions, charities and community organisations. We welcome conversations about developing programmes that respond to specific needs, communities and shared aims.
