Dramatherapy Bay

A space to pause, explore your inner world, and rediscover your path forward.

Providing dramatherapy and creative psychological support in Cambridge and online.

At Dramatherapy Bay, we believe that not all feelings and experiences can be fully captured by words alone. Through dialogue, creative exploration, and embodied experience, we offer a thoughtful, accepting, and non-judgmental space for you to process your experiences, explore your emotions, and re-understand yourself from new perspectives.

We offer diverse services for individuals, groups, and organizations, spanning personalized therapeutic support, creative well-being workshops, and collaborative projects.

The Philosophy Behind Dramatherapy Bay

A sheltered bay to pause, a space to explore self, and a journey to set sail anew.
