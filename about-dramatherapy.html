About Dramatherapy

"Sometimes, words are not enough to express what we feel or make sense of what we experience."

Dramatherapy offers another way to explore ourselves through creativity, imagination and expression. Alongside talking, we can use images, objects, stories and other creative materials to discover different perspectives and develop a deeper understanding of ourselves.

What is Dramatherapy?

Dramatherapy is a form of psychological therapy using creativity, imagination, play, storytelling and other expressive approaches within a safe, holding relationship.

✓

No Acting Experience

There is zero expectation to perform, act theatrically, or have any stage background.

✓

No Artistic Ability

You do not need to be good at drawing, crafting, or visual arts to benefit fully.

✓

Talking is Welcome

Conversation remains essential and central; creative modalities are always optional.

Creative Modalities

What might we use?

A range of expressive tools to help externalize and explore inner experiences safely.

Sandtray and Miniatures

Exploring thoughts, feelings and experiences through miniature objects and creating scenes in a tray of sand.

Images and Photographs

Using photographs, picture cards and visual prompts to explore emotions, memories and personal associations.

Clay and Creative Materials

Giving physical shape to feelings, experiences or ideas that may be difficult to express verbally.

Drawing and Painting

Using colours, shapes and images to explore emotions, experiences and different perspectives.

Storytelling and Story-making

Creating or exploring stories, characters and metaphors to approach personal experiences from a different perspective.

Poetry and Creative Writing

Using words, poetry and expressive writing to communicate thoughts and feelings.

The Process

How does a session work?

Every session is collaborative, flexible, and tailored entirely to your pace.

1

Arriving and Checking In

We begin with a conversation about how you are feeling, what has been happening and anything you would like to explore.

2

Exploring Together

Depending on your needs and preferences, we may use conversation, creative activities or a combination of both. There is no fixed formula.

3

Reflecting and Closing

We take time to reflect on what has emerged, consider what feels meaningful and gently bring the session to a close.

Why use creativity in therapy?

Sometimes, we may find that the way we consciously understand an experience does not fully capture how we feel about it. Through images, stories, objects and metaphors, we can explore different layers of our experiences and notice things that might otherwise be difficult to put into words.

Creative approaches offer another perspective and help us notice feelings, associations, memories and patterns that may not be immediately accessible through conversation alone.

Reassurance

Do I have to be creative?

"Absolutely not. You do not need to be artistic, imaginative or comfortable with performing."

Creative activities are simply additional ways of exploring and communicating. You are always free to choose whether you would like to try them. Some people prefer talking throughout their sessions. Others enjoy using creative materials, while many find a combination of both helpful.

We work at your pace and find an approach that feels comfortable for you.

Therapy beyond words.

Whether through conversation, creativity or a combination of both, Dramatherapy offers a space to explore, express and make sense of your experiences in ways that feel meaningful to you.
