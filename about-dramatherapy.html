About Dramatherapy

"Sometimes, words are not enough to express what we feel or make sense of what we experience."

Dramatherapy offers another way to explore ourselves through creativity, imagination and expression. Alongside talking, we can use images, objects, stories and other creative materials to discover different perspectives and develop a deeper understanding of ourselves.

What is Dramatherapy?

Dramatherapy is a form of psychological therapy using creativity, imagination, play, storytelling and other expressive approaches. It provides a safe container for self-exploration.

  • ✓ No acting experience needed — zero expectation to perform or be theatrical.
  • ✓ No artistic ability required — no need to be good at drawing or crafts.
  • ✓ Talking remains welcome — conversation is essential, and creative activities are always optional.
