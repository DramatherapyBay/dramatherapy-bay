Therapeutic Approach

What is Dramatherapy?

Dramatherapy is a specialized form of psychotherapy that harnesses the power of drama, theater, and creative expression to promote healing and personal growth.

A Holistic Approach to Healing

Unlike traditional talk therapy, dramatherapy engages the whole person – mind, body, and spirit – creating opportunities for profound healing and self-discovery. Through the safe medium of creative play and storytelling, individuals can explore difficult emotions and develop greater self-awareness.

This innovative therapeutic approach allows clients to safely explore their emotions, experiences, and relationships through techniques such as role-play, improvisation, storytelling, and creative movement. The therapeutic process is conducted by trained and licensed professionals who work collaboratively with clients to create a safe and supportive environment.

Evidence-Based Practice

Dramatherapy is recognized by the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC) and is used in hospitals, schools, community centers, and private practice settings worldwide.

Creative Modalities

What might we use?

A range of expressive tools to help externalize and explore inner experiences safely.

Sandtray and Miniatures

Exploring thoughts, feelings and experiences through miniature objects and creating scenes in a tray of sand.

Images and Photographs

Using photographs, picture cards and visual prompts to explore emotions, memories and personal associations.

Clay and Creative Materials

Giving physical shape to feelings, experiences or ideas that may be difficult to express verbally.

Drawing and Painting

Using colours, shapes and images to explore emotions, experiences and different perspectives.

Storytelling and Story-making

Creating or exploring stories, characters and metaphors to approach personal experiences from a different perspective.

Poetry and Creative Writing

Using words, poetry and expressive writing to communicate thoughts and feelings.

Who Can Benefit from Dramatherapy?

Dramatherapy is effective for individuals of all ages dealing with a wide range of emotional, behavioral, and mental health challenges.

✓ Addiction recovery support
✓ Bereavement and grief processing
✓ Eating disorder treatment
✓ Personality disorder therapy
✓ Anxiety and depression management
✓ Cognitive impairments and dementia care
✓ Learning disability support
✓ Trauma and PTSD healing
✓ ADHD and autism spectrum support
✓ Communication disorder assistance
✓ Life transition guidance
The Process

How does a session work?

Every session is collaborative, flexible, and tailored entirely to your pace.

1

Arriving and Checking In

We begin with a conversation about how you are feeling, what has been happening and anything you would like to explore.

2

Exploring Together

Depending on your needs and preferences, we may use conversation, creative activities or a combination of both. There is no fixed formula.

3

Reflecting and Closing

We take time to reflect on what has emerged, consider what feels meaningful and gently bring the session to a close.

Therapy beyond words.

Whether through conversation, creativity or a combination of both, Dramatherapy offers a space to explore, express and make sense of your experiences in ways that feel meaningful to you.
