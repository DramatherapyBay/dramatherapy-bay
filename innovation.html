Future-Focused Practice

Innovation, Research & Partnerships

Exploring new possibilities at the intersection of therapy, creativity and technology

At Dramatherapy Bay, we believe that psychological support should remain open to new ideas, perspectives and possibilities.

Alongside our therapeutic practice, we are interested in exploring how creative approaches, immersive technologies and emerging digital tools might contribute to emotional wellbeing and psychological support.

Our interest lies not in technology replacing human connection, but in understanding how it might offer additional ways for people to engage with their experiences, develop emotional awareness and explore different ways of relating to themselves and the world around them.

Exploring Virtual Reality in Therapeutic Practice

We are exploring the use of virtual reality (VR) as an additional resource within therapeutic practice.

Where appropriate, guided VR experiences may be incorporated into sessions as an optional, therapist-facilitated resource. Across different therapeutic approaches, including dramatherapy, CBT-T and Guided Self-Help, we are interested in how immersive experiences might create opportunities to explore sensory awareness, grounding, relaxation and emotional regulation.

We recognise that technology is not suitable for everyone. Its use should be guided by individual needs, preferences, accessibility and clinical suitability, with the therapeutic relationship remaining central to the work.

Academic Collaboration and Project Development

We are developing our interests in this area through collaboration with academic partners, exploring opportunities to bring psychological practice, research and technological innovation together.

Our developing areas of interest include:

Emotional regulation and anxiety management

Exploring creative and immersive approaches that may support emotional awareness and the management of anxiety.

Immersive and experiential technologies

Investigating how virtual environments and interactive digital experiences might complement psychological and therapeutic approaches.

Creative approaches to mental wellbeing

Exploring the relationship between dramatherapy, embodied experience, creativity and emerging technologies.

Applied research and development

Connecting academic knowledge with the practical experiences and needs of individuals, practitioners and communities.

These areas are part of an evolving programme of exploration and development. We are interested in building collaborations that allow ideas to be explored, evaluated and developed responsibly.

Working Together

We welcome conversations with universities, academic researchers, technology developers, healthcare professionals, charities and organisations interested in developing innovative approaches to mental wellbeing.

We are particularly interested in collaborative projects that bring together different areas of expertise, combining clinical and therapeutic perspectives with research, technology and real-world application.

Whether you are interested in research collaboration, project development, funding opportunities or exploring a shared idea, we would be pleased to hear from you.

Looking Towards What Is Possible

We see innovation as an ongoing process of curiosity, reflection and collaboration. By bringing together therapeutic knowledge, creative practice and emerging technology, we hope to contribute to new ways of understanding and supporting emotional wellbeing.

Interested in collaborating with us?

We welcome enquiries about research, partnerships and future projects.
