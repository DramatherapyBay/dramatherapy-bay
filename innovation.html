Innovation, Research & Collaboration

Exploring new possibilities at the intersection of therapy, creativity, and technology

At Dramatherapy Bay, we believe that psychological support should not be constrained by conventional methods alone, but should remain open to embracing new ideas, perspectives, and possibilities.

Alongside our day-to-day therapeutic practice, we actively explore how creative approaches, immersive technologies, and emerging digital tools can bring new dimensions to emotional well-being and psychological support.

Our focus is never on replacing human connection with technology, but rather on exploring how technology can serve as an additional supportive pathway—offering people more opportunities to connect with and understand their experiences, enhance emotional awareness, and discover new ways of relating to themselves and the world around them.

Exploring the Application of Virtual Reality in Therapy

We are currently exploring how Virtual Reality (VR) can be integrated as a supportive resource within different therapeutic contexts.

When appropriate, therapists may introduce guided VR experiences during sessions as a voluntary supportive tool. Whether in Dramatherapy, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Eating Disorders (CBT-T), or Guided Self-Help (GSH), we are interested in how immersive experiences can create additional exploratory opportunities—such as enhancing sensory awareness, practicing grounding and embodiment, promoting relaxation, and exploring emotional regulation strategies.

We recognize that technology-assisted approaches are not suitable for everyone. Therefore, the use of VR is always subject to individual needs, personal preference, practical accessibility, and clinical suitability, keeping the therapeutic relationship at the core of the entire process.

Academic Collaboration & Project Development

Through exchanges and partnerships with academic collaborators, we are gradually expanding these explorations, seeking opportunities to bridge psychotherapy practice, academic research, and technological innovation.

Ongoing areas of exploration include:

Emotional Regulation & Anxiety Management

Exploring how creative and immersive interventions can help enhance emotional awareness and support anxiety management.

Immersive & Experiential Technologies

Investigating how virtual environments and interactive digital experiences can complement existing psychological and therapeutic approaches.

Creative Approaches to Mental Health

Examining the relationship between dramatherapy, bodily experience, creative expression, and emerging technologies, and their potential applications in mental health support.

Applied Research & Project Development

Connecting academic knowledge with practical application, bridging research outcomes with the real-world needs of individuals, practitioners, and communities.

These directions remain part of an ongoing process of exploration and development. Through cross-disciplinary collaboration, we hope to allow new ideas to be further examined, evaluated, and developed responsibly.

Working Together

We welcome universities, academic researchers, tech developers, healthcare professionals, charities, and organizations to connect with us and explore innovative ways of supporting mental health together.

We are particularly interested in collaborative projects that combine cross-disciplinary expertise—uniting clinical and therapeutic perspectives with academic research, technological innovation, and practical application.

Whether you are interested in discussing research partnerships, co-developing projects, exploring funding opportunities, or simply exchanging ideas on a shared vision, we would love to hear from you and explore potential collaborations.

Looking Towards What Is Possible

We believe innovation is a continuous process of exploration, reflection, and collaboration. By integrating therapeutic expertise, creative practice, and emerging technologies, we aim to open up new pathways for understanding and supporting emotional well-being, shaping the future of psychotherapy and well-being services.
